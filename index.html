THE ART OF PERFUMERY

More Than a Fragrance. A Signature.

Founded in Grasse and refined in Paris, ÉLORA FRAGRANCES was created on a single belief: scent is the most intimate form of memory. We combine centuries-old French steam distillation with modern accord synthesis.

Every bottle is hand-poured in small batches, utilizing pure flower absolutes, ethically harvested agarwood, and organic cold-pressed citrus essences.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our extraits

What concentration are ÉLORA fragrances?
All our creations are formulated as Extrait de Parfum (25% to 30% concentration), ensuring exceptional depth, sillage, and longevity on the skin lasting upwards of 12-16 hours.
Are your perfume ingredients cruelty-free?
Yes, 100% cruelty-free. We strictly do not test on animals, nor do we source ingredients from suppliers that engage in animal testing.
How does international express shipping work?
We offer complimentary courier express shipping worldwide on orders above $150. Delivery takes 2-4 business days, fully tracked and insured in temperature-controlled packaging.
